# Business Strategy: Underwriting as a Service (UaaS)

This document outlines the commercial strategy, competitive landscape, and economic model for the MCP Sovereign Underwriting Engine.

## 💰 Business Model

We leverage a three-tiered economic model designed to align our revenue with the success of our merchants and the efficiency of our underwriters.

### 1. The Core SaaS Model (Platform Access)
*   **Per-Application Fee**: A low-margin "Utility" fee for every KYC/KYB screening run through the Gateway.
*   **Monthly Per-Merchant Active**: A recurring fee for the ongoing monitoring and AML screening of "Active" merchant accounts.

### 2. The Intelligence Premium (Value-Add)
*   **Atlas Performance Fee**: A premium charge for every "Healed" application. This is charged when Atlas successfully resolves a data gap that would have required a human staff hour.
*   **Forensic "Eye" Credit**: Usage-based credits for the multimodal vision-processing of storefronts and physical documents.

### 3. The Yield Sharing Model (Incentive Alignment)
*   **Router Optimization Percentage**: We capture a small percentage of the "found money" generated by the **Profit-Aware Router**. By routing a merchant to a bank with 20bps better margin, we share in the created EBITDA.

## 🎯 Strategic Differentiators

### The "Sovereign" Advantage
Unlike competitors who offer "one-size-fits-all" risk scoring, MCP allows partners to define their own **Cognitive Playbooks**. We provide the infrastructure (The Gateway, The Router, The Boarding Bridge), but the *Logic* belongs to the stakeholder.

### Profit-Aware Routing vs. Vertical Locking
Most modern providers (Stripe, Adyen) vertically lock merchants into their specific processing rails. Our **Profit-Aware Router** remains bank-agnostic, choosing the best processor based on current appetite and margin potential, essentially creating a "Stock Exchange" for merchant risk.

### Data Evolution
By capturing decision rationales in our **Context Graphs**, we aren't just storing merchant data—we are building a proprietary **Risk Intelligence Asset**. Over time, our "Precedent Engine" will identify industry-wide risk patterns that legacy text-based systems will consistently miss.
